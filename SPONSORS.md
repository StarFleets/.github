# Sponsors

StarFleets is kept going by its sponsors. To become one, go to [starfleets.net](https://starfleets.net).

## Logo rules

- A sponsor's mark is shown at its own colours, never recoloured, stretched or placed on a busy background.
- Marks sit in the profile's Sponsors section, one line each, in the order sponsors joined.
- We show a mark only with the sponsor's agreement and remove it on request.
- A sponsor's mark does not change anything in the project: sponsoring buys no say in decisions, ranks or content.
